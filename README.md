IKEA Gifting Customer Analytics & RFM Segmentation

1. Project Overview

This project focuses on customer analytics and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation using two years of online retail transaction data.

The business scenario is based on IKEA Gifting, a UK-based online retailer specializing in occasion gifts and home decor. The objective is to understand customer value, identify churn risk, and help the Marketing, CRM, and Retention teams make data-driven decisions.

The analysis covers customer purchasing behaviour from December 2009 to December 2011.

Dataset note: This project uses the UCI Online Retail II dataset, sourced from the UCI Machine Learning Repository and donated by Daqing Chen. IKEA Gifting is the business scenario used for the analysis. The dataset does not contain actual IKEA transaction records.

2. Business Problem

The business has historical transaction data but lacks a structured, data-driven approach to understanding customer value and identifying customers who may stop purchasing.

The analysis aims to answer:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers are at risk of churning?
- How does customer value and retention change over time?
- How do customer value and churn risk differ across geographic markets?
- What actions should the business take for each customer segment?

3. Business Objectives

- Identify high-value customers and customer segments.
- Segment customers using the RFM methodology.
- Quantify customer churn risk using a fixed 90-day inactivity threshold.
- Analyze customer value and revenue trends over time.
- Compare customer behaviour across geographic markets.
- Provide actionable recommendations for customer retention.

7. RFM Methodology

RFM analysis evaluates customers using three dimensions:

Metric| Definition
Recency (R)| Number of days since a customer's most recent purchase
Frequency (F)| Number of distinct purchase transactions made during the analysis period
Monetary (M)| Total revenue generated by a customer during the analysis period

Customers receive RFM scores based on their purchasing behaviour.

8. Customer Segmentation

The analysis classifies customers into six segments:

Segment| Business Definition
Champions| High recency, high frequency and high monetary value
Loyal| Consistent purchase history with moderate to high value
At Risk| Previously valuable customers whose purchasing activity has recently dropped
New| Recent first-time customers not yet established as repeat buyers
Needs Attention| Moderate recency but low purchase frequency
Lost| No meaningful activity for an extended period

9. Churn Identification

A fixed 90-day inactivity threshold is used to identify churned customers.

Customers with no recorded purchase activity in the trailing 90 days are flagged as churned.

The analysis uses this fixed threshold for simplicity and interpretability. It is a historical inactivity measure, not a predictive churn model.

12. Assumptions & Limitations

- Churn is defined using a fixed 90-day inactivity threshold rather than a statistically derived cutoff.
- Wholesale and individual retail customers are not separated unless the underlying data clearly distinguishes them.
- The analysis reflects historical transaction data only.
- External factors such as promotional calendars and seasonality drivers are not included.
- The analysis does not include predictive churn modelling or automated customer scoring.
